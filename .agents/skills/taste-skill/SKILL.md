# Taste Skill

## Rules
- Design must look like a Tesla showroom — expensive, calm, and cinematic.
- Colors: Obsidian Black `#080809` + Gold `#D4AF37` only.
- Headings: Fraunces serif font (`font-display`).
- Spacing: Lots of empty space and calm breathing room.
- Aesthetics: Absolutely no AI-looking purple/blue gradients or tacky effects.
